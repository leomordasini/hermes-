Setup — Before posting anything

▾
  • 1
    Create TikTok Business Account as @aljibeshop Settings → Manage Account → Switch to Business. Category: "Retail & E-Commerce". This unlocks TikTok Shop registration and analytics.
  • 2
    Register at seller.tiktok.com under MordasinLabs LLC Have ready: EIN, government ID, bank account details. Upload all 38 product listings with photos and prices. Approval takes 1–3 business days.
  • 3
    Register aljibe.shop domain (~$3 at Cloudflare) Just park it for now — no website needed yet. Having the domain establishes the brand and prevents squatting.
  • 4
    Write the TikTok bio "🕯️ Hand-poured desde Buenos Aires | Miami-made | Shop below 👇 | Tienda en español también 🇦🇷" — bilingual, location-coded, direct CTA.
  • 5
    Physical setup in her workspace Phone mount (overhead or 45° angle), one LED panel light aimed at the work surface, clip-on lapel mic. Total ~$80. See gear list below.

    File USPTO trademark for "Aljibe" — Class 4 tmsearch.uspto.gov → TEAS Plus filing → ~$250. The name is clear right now. File before you post publicly so it's on record.

This is the only gate. 1,000 followers = TikTok Live unlocked. Everything else waits here.

2
First Lives + First Sales
Weeks 4–8 · Starts at 1,000 followers · Target: $180–$270/month
▾
  • 1
    Start with 3 Lives per week, 90 minutes each Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday at 7 PM EST. Prime impulse-buy time. Ramp to daily once comfortable.
  • 2
    The "Pour With Me" format — no script needed Set up workspace, go Live, make candles while talking. Narrate the process: wax temperature, why this scent, what occasion it's for. Pin top 2–3 products to the Live Shopping cart.
  • 3
    One Live-exclusive deal every session "Anyone watching right now gets free shipping on orders over $30." FOMO is the most effective conversion lever during a Live. Never offer the same deal twice in a row.
  • 4
    Alternate English and Spanish Lives English Live Tuesday, Spanish Live Thursday ("¡Hola familia! Hoy hacemos velas de vainilla y coco…"). This double-dips the algorithm and taps an underserved audience.
  • 5
    Tease every Live 2 hours before in a short video "Going Live tonight at 7 PM EST — making the Buenos Aires collection 🕯️🇦🇷 — exclusive deal for anyone who joins." This primes viewers and boosts peak concurrent count.
3
Consistency Engine
Months 2–3 · Daily Lives · Target: $500/month
▾
  • 1
    Systematize content batching Sunday = film day. 30 min captures enough clips for the full week of short-form. Lives do the selling. Short videos keep the follower funnel flowing.
  • 2
    Introduce seasonal/limited drops during Lives "I only made 20 of this scent — once they're gone, they're gone." Scarcity during a Live is the single most effective conversion lever. Tie to holidays: Día de los Muertos, Navidad, Valentine's, Mother's Day.
  • 3
    Cross-promote Etsy for custom orders only During Lives: "For personalized labels or custom scents for weddings, visit my Etsy — link in bio." Keeps both channels earning without cannibalizing each other.
  • 4
    Track weekly: peak concurrent viewers, sales per Live, AOV TikTok's LIVE analytics show PCV and GMV per session. Aim for 50+ PCV as baseline. 200+ means breakout. Adjust stream time if numbers are low.
4
Scale Beyond $500
Months 3–6 · Affiliates + Bundles · Target: $1,500–$3,000/month
▾
  • 1
    Open TikTok Shop Affiliate Marketplace Other TikTokers promote Aljibe products for 10–15% commission. Zero upfront cost — only pay on sales. One video from a 50K creator can generate $500–$2,000 in a day.
  • 2
    Create Live-exclusive bundles "The Buenos Aires Set" — 3 candles + matchbox + gift wrap, $55. Higher AOV means fewer sales needed to hit revenue targets. Bundles don't exist on Etsy.
  • 3
    Dedicated weekly Spanish Live At 5,000+ followers, promote a dedicated weekly Spanish Live. Collaborate with other Latina makers — jewelry, bath & body, art — for joint Lives. Cross-audience exposure with no ad spend.
  • 4
    Request @aljibeshop on Instagram Once established on TikTok, report the dead Instagram account for inactivity via Instagram's trademark/impersonation claim flow. High success rate for clear inactive accounts.
