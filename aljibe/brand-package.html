Brand Concept

Where Buenos Aires lives in every flame

The philosophical foundation of the brand identity.

An aljibe is the stone well at the heart of a Buenos Aires courtyard — cool, deep, and full of something precious. It holds water the way a candle holds scent: quietly, patiently, until someone needs it.

Every Aljibe candle is a vessel. What lives inside it is memory — the jacarandas in bloom along Palermo streets, rain on colonial cobblestone, the kitchen of someone's abuela on a Sunday afternoon.

Hecho con amor. Desde Buenos Aires hasta Miami.

Artisan Heritage
Hand-poured, small-batch, made from scratch. The same care a Buenos Aires craftsperson has always brought to their trade.
Dual Identity
Argentine roots. Miami soul. Bilingual by nature — as comfortable in English as in Spanish, at home in both worlds.
Memory as Product
A candle's true value is intangible. Scent unlocks memory. Aljibe sells the feeling, not just the wax.
Stone & Fire
The well is stone: ancient, grounded, enduring. The flame is fire: alive, warm, ephemeral. Together they are Aljibe.
Color Palette

Six colors. One world.

Usage Guidelines

How to use this brand.

A small set of rules that keep Aljibe looking like Aljibe everywhere it appears.

✓ Do
✓Use the mark on dark, cream, navy, or terracotta backgrounds
✓Keep generous clear space around the logo — at least the height of the flame icon on all sides
✓Use Cormorant Garamond for all headings and the brand name
✓Use the gold variant for packaging, gift cards, and premium touchpoints
✓The stone ring segments can alternate terracotta and piedra stone
✓Bilingual copy — Spanish first when speaking to Spanish-speaking audiences
✗ Don't
✗Don't stretch, rotate, or distort the mark at any angle
✗Don't place the logo on busy photo backgrounds without a scrim or overlay
✗Don't use the wordmark in lowercase — it is always ALJIBE in caps with letter-spacing
✗Don't change the flame color — the terracotta flame is a fixed brand element
✗Don't add drop shadows to the logo mark
✗Don't use any typeface other than Cormorant Garamond + Inter — no exceptions
