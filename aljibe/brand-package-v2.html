Protect the mark

Usage Guidelines

What keeps the brand coherent as it grows.

✦ Do

Use the full primary logo on all physical packaging — never crop or scale below minimum sizes.
Use Cinzel Decorative for all "ALJIBE" wordmark applications. No substitutions.
Maintain parchment or navy backgrounds — the logo reads best on aged warm tones, not stark white.
Keep the gold outline present whenever the logo appears on dark surfaces.
Allow organic surface variation (wood grain, wax texture, parchment) under the logo — the brand was born from physical craft.
Use the badge mark (circular SVG) for profile images and wax seals — it scales down cleanly.
Code-switch freely in captions. English and Spanish together are on-brand.
Show the real workspace — the pouring, the drying, the labeling. Authenticity is the product.

✕ Don't

Don't flatten or recolor the scrollwork. The ornate detail is the mark — removing it makes it generic.
Don't use the logo on pure white or neon backgrounds. The parchment aesthetic dies on clinical white.
Don't use sans-serif fonts (Helvetica, Arial, Montserrat) for brand headers. They break the Victorian voice.
Don't crop the circular frame — the border is structural, not decorative.
Don't use drop shadows on the wordmark. The period-accurate style doesn't use soft modern shadows.
Don't add emojis to the logo or label system — keep the mark clean. Use emojis only in social copy.
Don't refer to the creator by name on any official brand asset. Hands only. Story only.
Don't apply the logo to busy photography without a clean field or overlay for legibility.
